01 / Development vision

Income-restricted should not mean quality-restricted.

Income qualification is a rent framework—not a ceiling on dignity, design ambition, or the standard of care residents should experience.

Snow’s proposition is to pair attainable rents with the discipline of a Class-A-quality residential experience: livable homes, durable decisions, welcoming shared space, and a distinctly Florida response to shade, light, landscape, and everyday life. The design and program remain conceptual and will be refined with the development team.

The Snow proposition

Class-A living describes the standard of care. Income-qualified rents describe whom the housing is structured to serve. We do not believe those ideas are in conflict.

I

Resident dignity

Homes and common spaces considered through the experience of the people who use them.

II

Partner discipline

Early alignment among development, design, construction, and long-term operations.

III

Stewardship over spectacle

Quality measured by usefulness, durability, and care—not by expensive gestures.

02 / Residences

A higher standard of care, carried home.

The Class-A-quality aspiration should be felt in the ordinary moments: a useful plan, good light, durable finishes, and a calm place to return to. Final plans and specifications have not been established.

“A home should feel considered from the moment you arrive.”

  • 01 Efficient, comfortable living spaces
  • 02 Durable materials selected for daily life
  • 03 A calm, contemporary Florida character

03 / Amenities

The experience between the street and the front door.

The amenity story is not a checklist. It is a sequence of everyday moments that can make a community feel considered, connected, and at home in Florida.

These are design intentions for partner evaluation—not a final or committed amenity program.

01

Project overview

A compact community shaped around daily life.

Buildings, landscape, circulation, and shared space are envisioned as one considered whole—not as leftover pieces organized around a parking field.

Design intentA plausible, connected framework for approximately 120 residences.

02

Shared amenity

Resort-quality care, without resort excess.

A pool courtyard can offer shade, rest, conversation, and genuine connection— designed to serve residents well rather than perform luxury for its own sake.

Design intentA durable, inclusive shared setting with warmth and reasons to return.

03

Stewardship & wellbeing

Wellbeing rooted in the landscape.

A shaded trail, useful garden, and modest wellness pavilion can support movement, rest, and neighborly life while treating the landscape as a resource to steward.

Design intentUseful, climate-aware choices that improve daily life and honor place.

Program status

Amenity scope, locations, features, and specifications remain conceptual and subject to site selection, design development, feasibility, and approvals.

04 / Our mission

Stewardship made visible in the way we build.

Our Christian faith is not a decorative layer. It calls Snow Development Group to approach development with humility, integrity, and care for the people and resources entrusted to us.

Love of neighbor becomes practical when decisions protect dignity, partnerships remain transparent, resources are handled responsibly, and quality is pursued in service of residents rather than status.

Dignity
See the resident as a neighbor, never a demographic.
Service
Make expertise useful to the people and partners around the work.
Stewardship
Hold quality, cost, time, and trust with equal seriousness.
Humility
State the vision clearly and the current stage honestly.
