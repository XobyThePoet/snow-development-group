# Snow Development Group — Concept Image Set

These images are early-stage architectural visualization studies for a proposed approximately
120-residence, Class-A-quality, income-restricted multifamily community in Brevard County,
Florida. They do not depict a completed project, approved design, secured site, or final amenity
program. Keep a visible conceptual-imagery disclosure wherever they are published.

## Final assets

1. `snow-concept-01-exterior-hero.png` — exterior building and streetscape hero
2. `snow-concept-02-pool-amenity.png` — pool and outdoor amenity deck
3. `snow-concept-03-residence-interior.png` — kitchen and living residence interior
4. `snow-concept-04-wellness-trail.png` — clay running trail, garden, and wellness pavilion
5. `snow-concept-05-community-overview.png` — compact three-building community overview

All files are landscape PNGs generated with the built-in image-generation workflow. The first
overview draft was rejected because it suggested a substantially larger campus; only the corrected
compact overview is included here.

## Shared visual language

- Premium photorealistic architectural visualization, clearly conceptual rather than documentary
- Plausible four-story Florida-modern multifamily architecture
- Warm off-white mineral stucco, limestone-toned bases, charcoal metal screens and balconies
- Wood-tone soffits, deep shade, warm early-morning light, soft humid atmosphere
- Layered subtropical planting with restrained, responsible Class-A-quality character
- No project name, address, signage, logos, labels, watermarks, or claims of completion
- Avoid luxury-resort excess, high-rise glamour, black-and-gold styling, and cinematic fantasy

## Final prompt set

### 01 — Exterior hero

Create a wide pedestrian-eye-level, three-quarter streetscape view of a proposed approximately
120-residence multifamily community in Brevard County, Florida. Show plausible four-story,
courtyard-oriented Florida-modern architecture with warm off-white mineral stucco, light
limestone-toned base accents, charcoal metal details, deep shaded balconies and loggias, green
privacy screens, generous overhangs, wood-tone soffits, sabal palms, live-oak character, and a calm
pedestrian edge. Weight the building toward the center and right with calmer negative space at left
for website copy. Use warm early-morning Florida light and a soft humid atmosphere. The image
should feel high-quality, responsible, realistic, and slightly idealized as a conceptual developer
visualization—not a completed project. No text, logos, signage, address, watermark, impossible
structure, luxury-tower glamour, or resort excess.

### 02 — Pool amenity

Using the exterior hero as the definitive project reference, create a wide deck-height view of a
shared courtyard pool framed by the same four-story buildings, materials, balconies, screens,
soffits, planting, and lighting. Include a modest rectangular pool, shaded gathering area, durable
communal seating, quiet blue water, pale slip-resistant paving, and realistic subtropical landscape.
The space should feel calm, useful, inclusive, and Class-A-quality without reading as a luxury
resort. Keep circulation and pool proportions plausible. A few small indistinct residents may be
used only for scale. No cabana spectacle, infinity edge, water slide, rooftop pool, text, logos,
signage, address, or watermark.

### 03 — Residence interior

Using the exterior and pool views as project references, create a wide interior perspective of an
open but efficiently planned apartment kitchen and living room. Include pale warm-oak and off-white
cabinetry, understated light solid-surface counters, matte charcoal fixtures and hardware,
practical unbranded full-size appliances, resilient wood-look flooring, warm neutral upholstery,
linen curtains, thoughtful storage, and strong natural light. A shaded balcony or large window
should look toward the same subtropical landscape and project architecture. The home should express
a high standard of care without conspicuous luxury. Keep dimensions, cabinetry, clearances, and
circulation plausible. No people, brands, readable labels, marble waterfall island, gold fixtures,
mansion scale, text, or watermark.

### 04 — Wellness trail and garden

Using the first three views as project references, create a wide eye-level landscape view of a
gently curving compacted warm terracotta-clay running trail within the community. Lead the trail
through layered subtropical shade planting toward a modest community garden and simple charcoal
and wood-tone wellness pavilion. Include practical raised garden beds, seating or a rest point,
and a small area for stretching or low-key outdoor wellness, with glimpses of the same four-story
buildings. Use warm dappled early-morning light and realistic Florida landscape. A few small
indistinct residents may jog, walk, garden, or stretch only for scale. Keep accessibility, grading,
drainage, and circulation plausible. No stadium track, intensive gym equipment, spa spectacle,
golf-course landscape, text, logos, signage, address, or watermark.

### 05 — Community overview

Using the first four views as definitive project references, create a wide elevated three-quarter
overview of one compact development with exactly three primary four-story residential building
masses arranged around a central recreation commons. Integrate the modest pool courtyard, clay
running trail, community garden, shaded wellness pavilion, flexible green, pedestrian paths,
arrival drive, and a realistic but visually softened amount of surface parking. Preserve the same
warm off-white mineral stucco, limestone-toned base, charcoal screens and balconies, wood-tone
soffits, subtropical planting, and early-morning light. The scene should plausibly suggest roughly
120 residences without claiming a final design. Do not repeat buildings into the horizon or show
an apartment campus. No property boundaries, dimensions, labels, signage, address, logos, text,
watermark, giant resort pool, impossible density, or unrelated background buildings.
